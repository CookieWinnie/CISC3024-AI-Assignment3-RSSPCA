Actual findings affecting implementation:

- The source code places U on the other side of the inverse from the paper. An exact
  matrix identity shows the division-free forms are equivalent, including zero entries.
- The paper describes 7/3 per-subject recognition splits and z-scores, while the downloaded
  classify_RSSPCA.m uses a global approximately 2/3 split and mean-centering. This project
  follows the paper's stated classification protocol and records that discrepancy.
- The reference stops on relative L2 projection change although its theoretical objective
  uses L1. Both quantities and direction change are logged; the monitor is not treated
  as proof of constrained stationarity.
- RSSPCA loadings are nonorthogonal. Reconstruction therefore uses W pinv(W), a declared
  evaluation change, rather than silently applying the reference W W^T expression.
- ORL.mat was already 56 x 46, so no invented resizing operation or original-size
  data verification was claimed.

## Executed experiments and verification

2026, 3024 and 77. Each training partition was split into 200 inner-training and
80 validation images. Nine parameter pairs were scored at 20 components. Final
30-component models were evaluated at 5, 10, 20 and 30 components for all three methods.

Reconstruction used seed 2026, separate parameter selection, and separate model fitting.

PSNR were computed without clipping. Example images were fixed by subject IDs 1,11,21,31
